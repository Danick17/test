# Bestmark — Brand Guidelines v1.0

> **Every best. On the record.**

---

## 1. Brand foundation

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Bestmark** — your personal *best*, made a permanent *mark* on the record. Also reads as "benchmark," which is exactly what a verified PR is. |
| **Tagline** | *Every best. On the record.* |
| **Mission** | Give every endurance athlete a single, verified, comparable athletic identity. |
| **Positioning** | The trust layer of endurance sport: where official results become identity. Strava is where you train. **Bestmark is what you've proven.** |
| **Brand promise** | If it's on Bestmark with a mark, it happened — timed, official, yours. |
| **Personality** | Precise, warm, quietly competitive. A meticulous training partner who remembers every split — never a drill sergeant, never a bro. |

### Naming conventions
- Product: **Bestmark** (one word, capital B only). Never "BestMark," "Best Mark," or "BM."
- The verification emblem: **the Mark** (capital M).
- The normalized score: **Bestmark Score** (0–100).
- Profile handles: `bestmark.com/@handle`.
- A verified personal record: "a **marked** PR."

---

## 2. Logo

**Concept: the Finish Tick.** A checkmark whose long upstroke is a rising pace line ending in a chip-timing gate notch — verification and performance in one glyph.

- **Primary lockup:** Finish Tick + wordmark "bestmark" in lowercase display type, tick sitting in place of the tittle-height space before the "b".
- **App icon / avatar:** Finish Tick alone, Verified Green on Ink.
- **Clear space:** ≥ height of the tick's short stroke on all sides. **Minimum size:** 24 px icon, 96 px lockup.
- **Don'ts:** don't rotate, outline, add gradients or shadows, place on photography without the Ink scrim, or recolor outside the palette.

---

## 3. Color

Verification is the brand's core, so **green is the hero** — deliberately distinct from Strava orange, Garmin blue, and Athlinks red.

### Core palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#0F1E2E` | Primary background (dark-first UI), wordmark |
| **Verified Green** | `#00C982` | The Mark, CTAs, verified states — use sparingly so it always means something |
| **Paper** | `#F7F9FB` | Light surfaces, text on Ink |
| **Slate** | `#5C7080` | Secondary text, dividers, unverified/logged data |
| **Signal Gold** | `#FFC53D` | PR moments, records, celebration only |

### Sport accents (data viz & profile chips only — never for UI chrome)

| Sport | Hex |
|---|---|
| Run | `#FF6B5E` coral |
| Swim | `#3AA0FF` azure |
| Bike | `#FFB020` amber |
| Tri / multisport | `#8B5CF6` violet |

**Rules:** Verified Green appears only where verification or primary action lives. Unverified data is always Slate — the visual hierarchy *is* the trust model. Maintain WCAG AA (4.5:1) for text; all five core colors pass on Ink or Paper.

## 4. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **Space Grotesk** (600/500) | Athletic, technical, slightly retro-futurist |
| UI & body | **Inter** (400/500/600) | Workhorse legibility at data density |
| **Times & numbers** | **IBM Plex Mono** (500), tabular figures | Every race time, split, and score renders in mono — times are sacred data and must align in columns |

Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48. Race times display large: a PR is a headline, not a table cell.

---

## 5. Voice & tone

**Principles**
1. **Data-honest.** Never inflate. "Your 10K Bestmark Score rose 2.1 points" — not "You're crushing it!!!"
2. **Every athlete counts.** A 38-minute 5K PR gets the same typography as a 15-minute one. Age-grading language always frames upward ("72% — Regional Class").
3. **Precise, then warm.** Lead with the number, follow with the meaning: "1:29:47 — your first sub-1:30, four years in the making."
4. **Competitive, never cruel.** We celebrate rivalries; we never trash-talk on an athlete's behalf.

**Vocabulary:** say *marked, verified, on the record, best, progression*. Avoid *crushed it, beast mode, no excuses, grind*.

**Sample strings**
- Empty state: "No marked results yet. Search your name — your history may already be waiting."
- PR notification: "New mark: 21:04 (5K) — 68.2%. That's a lifetime best."
- Claim confirmation: "That's you? Claimed. 14 results just joined your record."

---

## 6. Imagery & iconography

- Photography: real age-group athletes mid-effort or at finish lines — rain, grimaces, all body types and ages. No stock-perfect models, no elite-only imagery.
- Duotone treatment: Ink shadows + Verified Green highlight for hero imagery.
- Icons: 2px stroke, rounded caps, geometric — matching Space Grotesk's construction. Sport icons use the sport-accent palette.

---

## 7. The Mark (verification emblem)

The single most protected asset. A small Finish Tick badge rendered in Verified Green beside any official, timing-sourced result.

- Never applied to self-reported data — no exceptions, including partners and paid accounts.
- Never used decoratively; it appears only adjacent to a verified datum.
- Third parties embedding Bestmark data must render the Mark unaltered (per API display policy).
